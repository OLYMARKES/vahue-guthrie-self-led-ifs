Approved direction · VAHUE × Guthrie Sayen · after July 29 call

This course is about Self.

Our mission is to give people a living, safe, and understandable entrance into IFS through the experience of Self. Not through protocol. Not through learning to conduct sessions. Not through deep trauma work.

A six-week Self-led introduction to IFS for life. People learn to access Self as awake awareness, speak the language of parts, practice the loving gaze of Self, and begin the shift from a thought-based operating system to an awareness-based way of being.

  • Parts

  • Protectors
  • Other people
  • Vulnerable parts
  • Life

Approved course direction

How we now see this program

“Add more Self to the world.”

Guthrie anchor

After the July 29 alignment call, this is no longer only a proposal. Guthrie reviewed the frame, approved the direction, and gave us the central story that should now carry the course and the landing page.

We are building a six-week course as a standalone Self-led entrance into IFS for life. Not as protocol training, and not as practitioner preparation, but as a living introduction to the modality through the centrality of Self.

At the center is a person learning, perhaps for the first time, to recognize access to Self as a living state, speak the language of parts, and meet themselves, their parts, and other people with the loving gaze of Self. Structure, safety, and practice exist only to make that experience clear, gentle, and stable.

Current working status: the frame is accepted. The next work is to develop the landing, Week 1 content, participant materials, practice design, dates, and Guthrie's exact live-call arc.

Central story from the call

Operating system 1.0 → operating system 2.0

Guthrie added the language that now gives the course its public story: we are helping people move from operating system 1.0 to operating system 2.0.

Operating system 1.0 is the familiar thought-based, parts-led way of interacting with the world: focused, sequential, task-driven, often tense. Operating system 2.0 is awareness-based and Self-led: panoramic, inclusive, relaxed, and able to hold many signals at once without losing clarity.

“When you shift into awareness, access to Self, things change. You enter the flow of your life. Rather than trying to tick every little box on your to-do list — by doing less, we become more efficient.”

Guthrie Sayen · July 29 alignment call
Course goal
  • Give people a direct taste of access to Self as awareness.
  • Help them return to this access more frequently and stay with it longer.
  • Teach parts language as a way to recover access to Self in ordinary life.

So the aim is not only to explain a model. The aim is to give participants enough lived contact with OS 2.0 that they can recognize it, trust it, and begin using it in daily moments: when a part is activated, when attention becomes too narrow, when a relationship becomes charged, or when life feels like a list of tasks to push through.

Landing thesis: we are not only teaching IFS concepts. We are inviting people to bring a capacity that is already present in the background into the foreground of everyday life.

Operating system 1.0

  • Thought-based and parts-led
  • Attention-heavy and sequential
  • Trying to level up by doing more
  • Useful, but with a ceiling

Operating system 2.0

  • Awareness-based and Self-led
  • Field-aware and panoramic
  • Focus held inside a wider field
  • Relaxed effectiveness in life

Definition

What this course is

“The goal of all inner work is to access Self.”

Guthrie anchor

It is a Self-led course. It does not exist to teach people how to “do IFS.” It exists so a person can come into real contact with what IFS is from the inside.

It is

  • A proper introduction to IFS as a modality
  • A first taste of access to Self as awake awareness
  • An understanding of why Self can heal parts
  • Learning to notice parts and speak parts language
  • Practice meeting oneself and others with access to Self
  • A clear sense of whether to go deeper — with a therapist, in community, or in training

It is not

  • A compressed practitioner training
  • A course in conducting IFS sessions
  • Deep trauma or exile work
  • A promise to heal trauma in six weeks
  • Peer therapy in pairs and triads

The promise we make to participants

What people can count on

“Love yourself and you can love the whole world.”

Guthrie anchor

In six weeks, a person gets to know IFS from the inside — through the experience of Self, the language of parts, and the practice of loving presence.

01 Access Self

Meet Self not only as an idea, but as awake awareness and a state of presence from which a person can be with themselves.

02 Begin speaking parts language

Notice inner voices, protections, and reactions without shame, fighting, or self-attack.

03 Understand the next step

Sense whether IFS resonates, and see where to go deeper: therapy, community, or training.

What we are not promising

We are not promising trauma healing in six weeks, we are not teaching people to lead IFS sessions, and we are not positioning this course as practitioner preparation.

Тезис для лендинга: мы не просто объясняем концепции IFS. Мы приглашаем людей вывести способность, которая уже есть в background, в foreground повседневной жизни.

Групповая практика

Что происходит между людьми

“Curiosity is a great doorway.”

Guthrie anchor

Название меняет то, как люди действуют внутри формата. Поэтому мы меняем название.

Не называтьГрупповая практика. Тройки, где люди практикуют IFS.
→
НазыватьПространства практики Self-led контакта.

Группы не практикуют «ведение сессий». Они практикуют доступ к Self, речь с доступом к Self, слушание из доступа к Self, замечание части и уважительный контакт с ней — без исправления другого человека, без углубления, без интерпретации.

Можно

  • Короткий presencing
  • «Какая часть сейчас здесь?»
  • «Как я отношусь к этой части?»
  • Говорить от имени части
  • Говорить с доступом к Self
  • Слушать из доступа к Self
  • Благодарить защитника
  • Практика фраз для отношений
  • Круг интеграции

Нельзя

  • Терапия равных
  • Вести другого человека
  • Работа с изгнанниками
  • Unburdening
  • Копаться в воспоминаниях
  • «Иди глубже»
  • Советы, замаскированные под IFS
  • Интерпретировать систему другого человека
  • Давление делиться уязвимостью
