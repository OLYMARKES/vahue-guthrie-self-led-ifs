Self-led introduction to IFS for life

Course content skeleton

A six-week path into more access to Self.

This is the working course content layer after Guthrie's feedback: an experience-first introduction to IFS where participants meet Self as awake awareness, learn the language of parts, and practice a loving gaze toward themselves and others without peer therapy or forced depth.

6weekly live calls with Guthrie
90minutes: meditation, teaching, demo, Q&A
1lean handout per week, 4-6 points
0peer exile work, unburdening, protocol training
Course goal

Increase the frequency and duration of access to Self so participants can carry more awareness, compassion, and choice into ordinary life.

Guthrie anchor

"What we're doing is bringing something that's already present, but in the background, into the foreground."

Story engine

OS 1.0 is thought-based, parts-led, attention-heavy. OS 2.0 is awareness-based, Self-led, panoramic, and able to include more without tightening.

01 / Working promise

What participants are actually receiving

This course should feel like a guided entry into another way of being, not a compressed training. The participant leaves with language, experience, and practices they can use safely in life.

Experience before theory

A felt sense of Self

Participants taste Self through ground, core, field, focus; attention vs awareness; and simple pauses that bring awareness forward.

A language for parts

They learn to say "a part of me..." and create enough distance to meet anxiety, criticism, control, avoidance, or shame with curiosity.

A safer relationship with depth

They learn that protectors need respect and permission. Vulnerable material is witnessed gently and referred onward when therapy is needed.

02 / Intro guide

The pre-course orientation people receive

A short, readable guide before Week 1. It should lower shame, set safety, and give the minimum IFS map needed to participate without overload.

Participant-facing
Block Core content Guthrie voice / quote Participant takeaway
Why this course existsOS 1.0 to OS 2.0 Life run only by thought and effort reaches a ceiling. IFS gives a map for bringing awareness and Self leadership forward. "You enter the flow of your life. By doing less, we become more efficient." There is another way to function besides trying harder.
What Self means hereAwake awareness Self is not a performance state. It is accessible as awareness, presence, compassion, clarity, and inner spaciousness. "Self is a space to which we can gain more and more access." I do not need to be perfectly "in Self"; I can ask how much access is available.
What parts areInner multiplicity Parts are natural. They have positive intentions, histories, fears, and hopes. They are not the enemy. "All parts are good - even though some parts have bad roles." My reactions are not proof that I am broken. They are parts trying to help.
How we practice safelyBoundaries We practice accessing Self, speaking on behalf of parts, listening without fixing, and gratitude toward protectors. "We will do nothing without your permission." This is not peer therapy or trauma work. Depth is governed by permission.

03 / Six-week arc

The curriculum spine

Each week has one focus, one guiding question, one live teaching/demo, one safe practice, and one lean handout. Theory is included only to support direct experience.

One focus per week
Week Theme Guiding question Core theory Safe practice
01Opening Self access Attention, awareness, and the first taste of Self. What changes when awareness comes forward? Self as awake awareness; attention as focus; awareness as field; parts as contents of consciousness. Ground-core-field-focus, peripheral vision, walking with open seeing/hearing/body.
02Language of parts Parts are not problems; they are inner people trying to help. Can I notice a part without becoming it? Multiplicity, all parts are good, Self as conductor, "a part of me..." language. Naming a part, asking willingness to be with it, offering curiosity or compassion.
03Everyday protectors Managers and firefighters in daily life, leadership, work, relationships. What is this protector trying to prevent or soothe? Protectors, happiness plans, fear-driven roles, inner polarization. Protector spotting, speaking on behalf of a protector, gratitude instead of pressure.
04Permission and trust Why depth comes through permission, pacing, and gratitude. What happens if I stop forcing my system? Six F's translated into self-inquiry; permission; trust the system; slow is fast. Thanking a protector, asking "what do you need me to understand?", stopping before pressure.
05Vulnerable parts Understanding exiles without making exile work a participant practice. How do we respect vulnerable material without pushing it open? Exiles as young vulnerable parts; witness, safety, child in distress, when to work with a therapist. Recognizing tenderness, finding support, safe-and-comfortable place if something opens.
06Self-led life Bringing Self leadership into relationships, family, leadership, and community. How does this become a practice of life? Internal and external systems; field awareness in relationship; Sangha/community. Relational awareness, "I notice a part...", integration plan, continuation path.

04 / Week cards

Detailed working skeleton

These cards are the first content scaffold for week guides, Guthrie live-call planning, and participant handouts.

01

Meeting Self through awareness

What changes when awareness comes forward?

"The other way is awareness, which is panoramic and inclusive."
Guthrie, July 29 call
Teaching core

Introduce the Model of Conscious Experience: attention as focus, awareness as field, and Self as awake awareness. Participants feel the shift before studying the idea.

Live call

Meditation with ground, core, field, focus; short teaching; fingers/peripheral vision exercise; Guthrie demo of increasing access to Self.

Handout

One-page map: OS 1.0 / OS 2.0, attention vs awareness, four building blocks, daily walking practice.

02

Learning the language of parts

Can I notice a part without becoming it?

"By noticing a part we're taking the first step to recovering access to Self."
Guthrie, quotes corpus
Teaching core

Parts are natural, normal, and often beneficial. A part has a perspective, a history, and a protective intention. Self can relate to parts with curiosity and compassion.

Live call

Guthrie demonstrates the simple move from "I am anxious" to "a part of me is anxious," then shows how that creates space.

Handout

Parts language dictionary: "a part of me...", "I notice a part that...", "How do I feel toward this part?", "Am I willing to be with it?"

03

Protectors in everyday life

What is this protector trying to prevent or soothe?

"Protectors, just like you and me, want to be seen and celebrated."
Guthrie, live sessions
Teaching core

Managers anticipate, control, and optimize to prevent pain. Firefighters react, soothe, distract, numb, or interrupt pain now. Both want the system's wellbeing.

Live call

Everyday examples: critic, perfectionist, overworker, people pleaser, distractor, rebel. Demo with a low-intensity protector, not deep trauma.

Handout

Protector spotting sheet: trigger, part voice, fear, noble purpose, what gratitude might sound like.

04

Permission, pacing, and gratitude

What happens if I stop forcing my system?

"We will do nothing without your permission."
Guthrie, Three Messages for Protectors
Teaching core

Translate the Six F's into self-inquiry. The essence is not protocol performance. It is respectful contact: find, focus, feel toward, befriend, and stop where permission stops.

Live call

Guthrie shows protector contact through gratitude and permission. The group watches how slow becomes efficient when the system is not pressured.

Handout

Permission script for self-practice: "Is it okay to stay here?", "What do you need me to understand?", "Thank you for trying to help."

05

Vulnerable parts without forcing depth

How do we respect vulnerable material without pushing it open?

"Find me. See me. Love me."
Guthrie on exiles
Teaching core

Explain exiles carefully as young vulnerable parts that may carry pain, shame, fear, loneliness, or hurt. This is orientation, not participant practice in exile work.

Live call

Guthrie may demonstrate how Self relates to vulnerable material safely. Participants learn witnessing and boundaries, not unburdening or guiding.

Handout

Safety guide: what to do if tenderness opens, how to find support, when to work with a therapist, how to stop and ground.

06

Self-led life and relationships

How does this become a practice of life?

"Field awareness is the perfect way to be in relationship with other people."
Guthrie, quote corpus
Teaching core

Bring IFS from inner work into life: relationship conflict, parenting, leadership, team polarization, self-compassion, and daily resets.

Live call

Integration meditation, relational demo, Q&A, and bridge into ongoing practice/community. If Guthrie wants, Week 6 can include a carefully held exile orientation demo.

Handout

"My life with more Self": personal map, signs of more access, next practice rhythm, therapist/community continuation.

05 / Practice design

What people practice between calls

Practice should train Self-access, language, and relational presence. It should not imitate practitioner sessions.

Solo daily reset

Ground, core, field, focus for 3-7 minutes. Track how much access to Self is available, without making it binary.

Parts noticing

Write one sentence: "A part of me..." Then add: "How do I feel toward this part?" No digging for memories.

Listening circle

One person speaks on behalf of a part. Others listen from as much Self-access as available, without fixing or interpreting.

Life experiment

Use field awareness in a real moment: walking, hard conversation, work pressure, parenting, conflict, or rest.

Allowed

  • Accessing Self and tracking degree of access.
  • Speaking on behalf of a part.
  • Listening without fixing.
  • Thanking protectors.
  • Relational awareness and integration circles.

Not allowed

  • Peer therapy or guiding another person's IFS process.
  • Exile work, unburdening, or memory digging.
  • Interpreting someone else's system.
  • Pressure to go deeper.
  • Advice dressed as IFS.

01 / Обещание

Что участник на самом деле получает

Курс должен ощущаться не как сжатое обучение методу, а как бережный вход в другой способ быть. Участник уходит с языком, опытом и безопасными практиками для жизни.

Опыт до теории

Живое ощущение Self

Участники пробуют Self через ground, core, field, focus; различение attention/awareness; и простые паузы, где awareness выходит вперёд.

Язык частей

Они учатся говорить "часть меня..." и создавать достаточно пространства, чтобы встречать тревогу, критику, контроль, избегание или стыд с любопытством.

Более безопасные отношения с глубиной

Они узнают, что protectors нуждаются в уважении и permission. Уязвимый материал встречается мягко и, когда нужно, ведёт к терапии.
